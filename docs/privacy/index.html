Domain Health Inspector

Privacy Policy

Domain Health Inspector is designed as a local-first domain asset inspection app. Domain lists, reports, and backups stay under your control.

1. Overview

Domain Health Inspector helps users inspect domain expiry, RDAP registration data, DNS records, HTTPS availability, TLS handshakes, and mail security records.

2. Data Stored on Device

The app may store domain names, service ports, usage profiles, inspection settings, report findings, report history, purchase entitlement state, language preference, and local backup metadata on your device.

3. Network Checks

When you run an inspection, the app connects to public DNS, RDAP, HTTP, HTTPS, TLS, and mail-related endpoints needed to produce the report. These checks are initiated by you or by app refresh behavior for domains you manage.

4. Backups and Exports

JSON backups and PDF reports are created only when you choose to export them. Exported files are handled by iOS document sharing and are your responsibility after export.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration is required for local use.
  • No domain portfolio is uploaded to a Domain Health Inspector cloud account.
  • No passwords, DNS provider credentials, registrar credentials, or private keys are requested.
  • No packet capture or browsing history is collected.

6. Support

If you contact support, include only the information needed to troubleshoot the issue. Do not send registrar passwords, DNS provider credentials, private keys, or other secrets.
